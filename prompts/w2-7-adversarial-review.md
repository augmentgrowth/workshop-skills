# W2 · Step 7 — The Adversarial Review

**Where:** the **same chat** as your announcement from [Step 6](w2-6-invoke-myvoice.md) — the reviewer needs it on the table.
**What you'll have when done:** a grounded critique of your announcement, checked against your real emails — not an opinion, evidence.

## Steps

1. Stay in the same chat as your announcement.
2. Paste the prompt box below.
3. Read the report — it should cite your actual emails by number. Give your verdict on which fixes to apply.

## The prompt — copy everything in the box

```text
Spin up a review subagent: compare this output against my real emails in email-context.md. Use named or quoted examples from the file to explain whether it is clear, concise, and matches how I actually write. Report what to change, then wait for my verdict before applying fixes.
```

## Done when

You've seen a diff grounded in your own writing, given your verdict, and watched the fixes land. This pattern — *spin up a subagent to adversarially review this* — works on anything you make, not just email.
