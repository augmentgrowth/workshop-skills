# Workshop 2 — The Interview (step 1)

Copy everything below the line and paste it into Claude Code (in your second-brain folder). Answer by voice.

---

Interview me, then write my working profile, so every future session starts already knowing me.

I'll answer by voice dictation — expect rambling, run-on answers and transcription typos. Pull the facts out of what I say. If a name, acronym, or product name looks garbled or unusual, confirm the spelling before you write it down.

HOW TO RUN THE INTERVIEW
- Ask ONE question at a time, numbered, then wait for my answer.
- If an earlier answer already covered a later question, skip that question — never ask for something I already told you.
- If I say "skip," move on immediately.
- At most one short follow-up per question, and only when something important is unclear (a name, an acronym, who judges my work).
- No commentary between questions — no "great answer!" — just the next question.

THE QUESTIONS — durable facts only, not my current projects
1. Your name, role, and company — and what the company actually does, in your own words.
2. What does your team do, and who's on it? Names and what each person owns.
3. Who else do you work with constantly — your boss, stakeholders, partners, clients, vendors? Who do you answer to?
4. How is your job judged? What does "doing well" look like — numbers, deliverables, or someone's opinion?
5. What do you produce most weeks — the recurring outputs (reports, decks, emails, schedules, analyses)?
6. What acronyms, product names, or internal shorthand would an outsider not understand? Define each in a few words.
7. What tools do you live in daily, and where do important files live?
8. Where are you located, what's your time zone, and what are your working hours?
9. How should Claude communicate with you — short bullets or full detail? Challenge you or just execute? What do AI answers do that annoys you?
10. What do you find yourself re-explaining over and over — to Claude, or to new coworkers?

WHEN THE INTERVIEW IS DONE, WRITE TWO FILES
1. Create `02_Areas/Identity/About Me.md` — my full profile. This replaces any older version.
2. Overwrite `CLAUDE.md` at the top level of this vault. Keep anything from the current file that is still true and not covered by the profile.

WHAT GOES WHERE — no overlap between the two files
- `CLAUDE.md`: who I am in 2–3 lines, how to communicate with me, any standing rules, and this pointer: "For full context about me, read `02_Areas/Identity/About Me.md`." Under 30 lines total.
- The profile file: everything else — team, stakeholders, how I'm judged, recurring outputs, vocabulary, tools, schedule. Under 60 lines. Start it with "Last updated:" and today's date.

WRITING RULES — strict
- Compressed reference notes, not prose. Short bullets and fragments. No intro or closing paragraphs, no restating my answers in fancier words, no heading with only one thin line under it.
- Only facts I actually said. No flattery, no "passionate about," nothing invented.
- Durable facts only: leave out anything tied to a current project, this quarter, or a rollout in progress — that belongs in project folders, not in my profile.
- Use my real vocabulary: actual names, actual acronyms each with a few-word definition.

When both files are written, show them to me, then finish with exactly this line:
"Prove it: start a new session and ask — what do you know about me?"
