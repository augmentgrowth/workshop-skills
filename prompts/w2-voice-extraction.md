# Workshop 2 — MyVoice: Extract Your Writing Samples (step 2)

Copy everything below the line and paste it into Claude Code (in your second-brain folder). Approve the plan it proposes.

---

Using my Microsoft 365 connector, build a writing-voice corpus from my sent email.

1. Search my Sent folder for roughly my last 100 sent emails. Only keep text I wrote myself — never quoted replies, forwarded text, or anything written by someone else.
2. Skip automated mail entirely: calendar invites and updates, invoices, receipts, notifications, mass mail.
3. Strip signatures, legal footers, and quoted chains from every keeper.
4. Privacy filter: skip emails about payments, contracts under negotiation, legal, HR, or health matters. If an otherwise-good email contains one sensitive detail, replace it with [redacted] instead of dropping the email.
5. Authenticity filter: if an email looks AI-written or heavily AI-polished, do not put it in the main corpus.
6. Aim for 15-25 keepers with real diversity: casual AND professional, long AND short, quick replies AND thought-out ones. If the corpus is thin, search further back until it isn't.
7. Save two files in my current folder:
   - email-context.md — the cleaned samples, numbered, each with a one-line label
   - review-uncertain.md — anything you weren't sure about (possibly AI-written or sensitive), each with a one-line reason and a KEEP / DROP checkbox for me
8. Finish by reporting: kept vs skipped counts, why you skipped what you skipped, and whether this corpus is strong enough to build my voice skill from.
