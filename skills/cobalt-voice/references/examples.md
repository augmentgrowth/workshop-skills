## 1. Voice calibration

**Opening a page for owners**
- Generic: "Cobalt is a leading acquirer of commercial access and security businesses with a differentiated operating model."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "Many business owners rightly fear the prospect of an acquisition. That's because too often employees and customers get lost in the shuffle when financial engineering takes precedence over mission and values."

**Explaining what changes after a deal**
- Generic: "We preserve and enhance the culture, brand equity, and operational identity of each acquired entity."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "You keep your brand, your team, and your culture – but now with more tools, training, and support to help you grow and deliver great service."

**Describing support services**
- Generic: "Our platform delivers comprehensive shared services across recruiting, technology, and finance."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "We work to remove roadblocks so you can focus on your customers and your craft."

**Naming the company's origin**
- Generic: "Cobalt reflects our commitment to strength, reliability, and enduring value."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "And we named ourselves Cobalt to honor the blue-collar workers who drive our businesses forward."

**Answering a customer's "what happens to us"**
- Generic: "Customers can expect a seamless transition with no material change to service delivery."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "In short, your customers remain yours – and Cobalt is here to help you take even better care of them."

**A technician-facing announcement**
- Generic: "As part of the integration, employees will gain access to enterprise-grade learning and development resources."
- Cobalt *(constructed)*: "Nothing about your day changes on Monday. Over the next few months you'll get access to training hubs and a national group of technicians who do exactly what you do."

**Declining to over-promise on valuation**
- Generic: "Our proprietary valuation methodology delivers maximum value for sellers."
- Cobalt *(actual)*: "Valuation is an art and a science, and we would be happy to walk you through our process in a direct conversation."

## 2. Thinking, not styling

**AI declaration:** "Cobalt's differentiated approach centers on people, culture, and long-term value creation, which drives superior outcomes for all stakeholders."

**Cobalt's reasoning** *(actual)*: "Our approach is different because it's people-first, and that ethos informs everything we do and build. We forge enduring partnerships with the people we serve… We treat our partner companies as our customers, not numbers on a spreadsheet. We're not buying businesses to flip them or fold them into a bureaucratic machine."

The AI version asserts the difference. Cobalt states the principle, then spends three sentences showing what it rules out. The negations are the argument.

**AI declaration:** "Post-acquisition, we work closely with owners to determine the optimal transition structure."

**Cobalt's reasoning** *(actual)*: "Your role post-acquisition depends on your goals. Some owners choose to stay on in an advisory or leadership role; others transition out over time. We don't believe in a 'one-size-fits-all approach' and will work with you to find the right path."

Cobalt answers the reader's actual question first, gives the two real options, and only then states the principle.

## 3. Costume versus natural

**Costume:** "As stewards of your legacy, Cobalt provides a long-term home where your craft, your people, and your local touch are honored behind the scenes by a people-first partner committed to unlocking possibility."

Every marker fires at once. Nothing is said.

**Natural** *(actual)*: "Cobalt provides behind-the-scenes support to supercharge the efforts of its partner companies, while preserving their local touch and feel."

Two markers, one claim, one qualifier. The identity comes from the reasoning, not the vocabulary.

**Costume:** "Unwavering stewardship means we do right by our partner companies, honoring legacies and enjoying the ride together as we unlock possibility."

**Natural** *(actual)*: "We show up humble, curious, and with the courage to change our minds."

## 4. Craft

**Opening**
- Before: "Cobalt is a long-term home for market-leading commercial access and security businesses, providing support while preserving what makes each business great."
- After *(actual)*: "Cobalt buys exceptional commercial access and security businesses."
- Six words of subject and verb. The qualifications come later, once the reader knows what this company does.

**Contrast**
- Before: "Cobalt is a long-term home that builds on what you have created rather than taking it apart."
- After *(actual)*: "Cobalt is a long-term home that amplifies — not dismantles — what you've worked so hard to build."
- One dashed contrast, placed where the fear lives. This is the sentence the em-dash budget exists for.

**Caveat that doesn't sound evasive**
- Before: "Brand consolidation may occur in certain circumstances at Cobalt's discretion."
- After *(actual)*: "In rare cases – like when we buy a small business in the same market as a larger one we already own – we may consolidate brands. But we make those decisions with care and focus on continuity and customer experience."
- The exception is named concretely enough to be checkable. Vagueness here would read as a trapdoor.

**Ending**
- Before: "We look forward to discussing how Cobalt can support your business."
- After *(actual)*: "We're happy to walk through specific details of our process in a direct conversation."
- Open door, no urgency, no summary of what was just said.
